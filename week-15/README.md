# Week 15 — Mission S29: Enterprise Compliance Gap Analysis Report

**Domain:** AI Governance and executive risk communication  
**Audience:** Board and investor due diligence  
**Source assessment:** [Week 14 Governance Gap Assessment](../week-14/governance-gap-assessment.md)

## Deliverables

1. [Enterprise compliance gap analysis report](./compliance-gap-analysis-report.md) — board-facing report with executive summary, four business-impact findings, regulatory exposure table, prioritised actions, owners, timeframes and measurable outcomes.
2. [Regulatory exposure table](./regulatory-exposure-table.md) — four findings mapped to named EU AI Act articles, with a legal-scope limitation.
3. [Recommended actions list](./recommended-actions.md) — four prioritised actions, each with an owner role, timeframe and measurable outcome.

An editable Word copy of the report is also supplied separately for review and editing. The mission brief says “Document” and does **not** require PDF format; therefore, PDF is not treated as a required submission format. The Markdown report is included here so the report can be read and edited directly in GitHub.

## Evidence basis and limitations

These artefacts were prepared from the supplied S28/S29 section notes, Evidence Brief B and the Week 14 NovaPay governance gap assessment. The assessment does not claim live production testing or establish the cause of either open incident. EU AI Act references are potential exposure areas only until legal/compliance confirms the intended use, affected persons and territorial scope.

**Scope:** Week 15 only. The portfolio's main README and all other weeks are outside this task.
